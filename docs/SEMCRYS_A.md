# SemCrys-A role/profile compiler — candidate control surface

`semcrys archaic profiles` emits a rooted profile registry. `semcrys archaic
plan SOURCE --role ROLE ...` compiles one read-only S > CM1 > CM2 requested
configuration against the current application. The final IC is created only by
realization, when concrete legend placement, BODY schema, and projection widths
or entry counts are known. The plan is SFTIR-compatible control notation;
the current `.crys` and `.leg` ABIs still perform the byte realization.

| Role | Current realization | Required shared state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SELF_CONTAINED_MANUAL | C:SELF_CONTAINED_TOKEN | none; local legend embeds |
| PENPAL_LONGITUDINAL | A:LONGITUDINAL_TOKEN_DELTA | at least one ordered prior legend |
| GUILD_PRESHARED | T:PRESHARED_TOKEN | exact guild/domain legend stack |
| HYBRID_GUILD_LONGITUDINAL | A:LONGITUDINAL_TOKEN_DELTA | base plus at least one adopted delta |

PACKED, INTEGER, and qualified TEMPLATE GLYPH projections are selectable.
Surface-unit meaning is executable. GCNL sense resolution and keyed local-ID
shuffle remain typed unavailable because the candidate packages neither a sense
resolver nor a cryptographic adapter. A role plan is not encryption and does not
turn local IDs into canonical CSIL.

Body fractionation (`second-pass`), multi-document pooling (`group-legend`),
cross-document recurring blobs, and legend recursion are separate mechanisms.
The planner does not collapse or infer them. To realize a plan, run ordinary
`encode` with the plan's profile, legend order, projection, policy and outer
codec, retain its full receipt, and verify the inverse. No shared relationship
state is adopted or mutated automatically.

The SFTIR addresses describe control dispositions only. Domain byte operations
remain the codec mechanism. Standing is `CANDIDATE_ONLY`; no security,
authority, release, admission, or canonical-mutation effect is claimed.
